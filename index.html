Monthly assumptions

The slabwise formula and 25-point eligibility are inferred from August and September 2026 statements, not confirmed as permanent terms. Both are subject to the same monthly 10,000-point cap as debit transaction rewards. October showed zero slabwise points after transaction rewards reached the 10,000-point cap; month-end credits can therefore be zero. Splitting ₹30,000 into three ₹10,000 transactions yields the same transaction points as one ₹30,000 payment, provided each payment qualifies. All calculations assume eligible debit-card transactions.
